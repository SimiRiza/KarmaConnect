# KarmaConnect — Brand Guidelines

## Positioning
KarmaConnect is a community-impact platform, not just a volunteering app.
It connects local problems with the people, skills, and funding needed to
solve them — and proves the impact once it's done.

Core line to use everywhere: "From local problems to real impact — together."

Avoid describing it as "an app to find volunteering opportunities" — that
undersells it. Always frame it as: Discover → Match → Contribute → Track Impact.

## Tone of voice
- Warm, human, and grounded — never corporate-SaaS-cold
- Confident but not salesy — this is a trust-driven platform, so avoid hype
  language ("revolutionary," "disrupting")
- Simple, plain language over jargon. If a CSR manager, an NGO coordinator,
  and a college student should all understand a sentence equally, that's the
  right register.
- Transparent and honest, especially around money — never vague about where
  funds go

## Color palette
- Primary: green/teal — represents growth and community
- Accent: warm orange/coral — used for CTAs (Sign Up, Contribute, Post a
  Problem) so action buttons stand out against the calmer primary tones
- Neutral backgrounds: warm off-white / light gray, not stark white — keeps
  the "community" feel rather than a clinical SaaS look
- Status colors (used consistently across all dashboards):
  - 🟡 Reported — yellow
  - 🔵 Team Forming — blue
  - 🟠 Funding Needed — orange
  - 🟣 In Progress — purple
  - 🟢 Completed — green

## Typography
- Headings: a clean, modern sans-serif with slightly rounded terminals
  (friendly, not sharp/corporate) — e.g. Poppins, Inter, or similar
- Body: highly readable sans-serif, minimum 16px on web for accessibility
- Avoid more than 2 typefaces total

## Logo & naming
- Product name is always "KarmaConnect" — one word, capital K and C, no space
- Tagline pairs with the logo: "From local problems to real impact — together."

## UI principles
- Every project/problem card shows: photo, location, category, urgency,
  status — consistently, in that visual hierarchy
- Money and impact numbers are never hidden behind extra clicks — transparency
  is a brand value, not just a feature
- Before/after photos are a first-class visual element wherever a completed
  project is shown
- Use icons sparingly and consistently: 🗺️ Discover, 🤝 Match, 💪 Contribute,
  📊 Track Impact — keep these four as the recurring visual language across
  marketing pages and in-app navigation

## Who we're speaking to (adjust tone slightly per audience, same core voice)
- NGOs/community orgs: practical, respectful of their existing on-ground
  expertise — KarmaConnect supports them, doesn't replace their judgment
- CSR companies: professional, data/impact-forward, credibility-focused
- Volunteers/students: encouraging, low-friction, makes helping feel easy
  and low-pressure (e.g. "One hour to help" framing, not guilt-driven)

## What to avoid
- Don't position citizens as passive "reporters" (that's the government
  complaint-app model) — always frame them as active problem-solvers
- Don't overuse gamification language (badges, streaks) — a light reputation
  system is fine, but this isn't a game
- Don't make verification/trust messaging feel bureaucratic — keep it
  reassuring, not intimidating
